# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Individual developers and OSS contributors — not enterprise buyers or decision-makers. They discover tools through GitHub, Hacker News, dev social media, conference talks, and word of mouth. They evaluate by reading code, issues, and commit history, not sales pages. They care about maintainer responsiveness, doc quality, and whether a project feels alive, and they often become informal internal champions who bring tools into their workplace.

## Product Purpose

Calloway Project is an open source organization building platform-engineering and developer-experience (DX) tooling — the infrastructure layer that sits underneath a team's daily workflow. The brand's job is credibility and legibility, fast: a developer should be able to tell within a minute what a tool does, whether it's actively maintained, and whether the people behind it know what they're doing. Success looks like trust earned through transparency and craft, not persuasion through marketing polish.

## Brand Personality

"The master craftsman's workshop" — plain-spoken, confident, understated, warm, direct about tradeoffs. Friendly because it's helpful and clear, not cutesy or gimmicky. The Art Deco-influenced mark (geometric, precise, built to last) signals engineered craft and permanence, not aggression or swagger. Voice talks to a developer, not at them; clarity does the work exclamation points usually try to do.

## Anti-references

Hype-driven SaaS marketing ("unlock next-generation platform synergy"), enterprise-grade/mission-critical/industry-leading buzzword stacking, "thought leader" framing, cutesy mascots or gimmicky illustration, generic emoji feature icons, stock "diverse team around a laptop" photography, soft pastel gradients standing in for real content, evasive marketing-speak, and any implication that there's one right way to build a platform.

## Design Principles

1. **Legible by design** — docs, source, and UI are meant to be read and understood, not just used. "You can see how it works" is itself a feature.
2. **Credibility over persuasion** — the site's job is to prove competence and activity fast, not to sell.
3. **Precision over ornament** — the Art Deco posture (geometric confidence, engineered permanence) governs layout and component rigor, not just the mark.
4. **Honest about maturity** — transparent about what's stable vs. experimental; "still early, rough edges welcome" beats overselling.
5. **Flexible, not opinionated** — good defaults, nothing bolted shut; every surface should feel reshapeable, not prescriptive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA color contrast is a hard requirement, certified separately for light and dark mode token sets (DESIGN.md → Color Palette, Dark Mode). Accent colors are chosen and flipped per-theme specifically to pass AA in both directions (text and button background) without per-state workarounds.
